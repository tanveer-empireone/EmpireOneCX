# Ava Knowledge Improvement Workflow

Ava does not learn automatically from visitor chats. Use this review loop to improve answers safely.

## Weekly Review

1. Review `data/ai-unanswered-questions/YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl`.
2. Review `data/ai-feedback/YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl` for down-rated answers.
3. Group repeated questions by topic.
4. Write one approved, accurate answer for each important topic.
5. Add only approved company knowledge to `data/ai-knowledge-base.php`.

## Rules

- Do not copy private visitor details into the knowledge base.
- Do not add pricing, guarantees, certifications, or timelines unless approved.
- If a question needs sales, legal, security, or custom pricing, Ava should collect contact details or hand off.
- Keep knowledge-base entries concise and factual.

## Useful Topics To Watch

- Pricing and proposals
- Compliance and security
- Service scope
- Hiring and career questions
- Locations and delivery centers
- Onboarding timelines
- AI capabilities
